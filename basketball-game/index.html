🏀 HOOPLAND

Professional Basketball Game

HOW TO PLAY 🎮

Controls:

W/A/S/D - Move your player

MOUSE - Aim at the hoop

CLICK - Shoot (hold for power)

SPACEBAR - Pass to teammates

SHIFT - Sprint (uses stamina)

P - Pause game

Objective:

Score 2-point baskets and outscore the opposing team!

Tips:

  • Aim for the center of the hoop
  • Manage your stamina wisely
  • Pass to open teammates
  • Play defense to prevent opponents from scoring
